How We Build Your Cake

Every custom cake follows the same five steps — whether it's a simple slice or a three-tier showstopper.

Fresh Ingredients
Baked to Order
Filled & Frosted
Hand-Piped Detail
The Reveal
Step 1

Fresh Ingredients

Real butter, real eggs, scratch-made batter. No mixes, no shortcuts — every cake starts the same honest way.

Step 2

Baked to Order

Nothing is baked ahead and frozen. Your cake goes in the oven for your date, then cools before we touch it.

Step 3

Filled & Frosted

Layers are filled, crumb-coated, and finished in Swiss meringue buttercream — smoothed by hand, not a machine.

Step 4

Hand-Piped Detail

Borders, bows, lettering, and sugar work — piped freehand to match your theme, down to the last detail.

Step 5

The Reveal

Boxed, stabilized, and handed over ready for its moment. This is the part we build the whole cake for.
